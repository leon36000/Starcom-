# STARCOM post-R0.1 sequence

R0.1 supplies a proof-gated core, not the complete STARCOM product.

## R0.2 — Governed C1 recovery envelope

- Ed25519 public-key registry with explicit owner acceptance.
- Exact-byte signature verification.
- Disposition-schema validation.
- Trust-root versioning and revocation.
- Recovery authorization distinct from recovery execution.
- Clean-room replay receipt.

The historical reviewer artifacts must be ingested by exact bytes; they may not be reconstructed from summaries.

## R0.3 — C2 recollection runner

- durable pre-request attempt record;
- monotone wave state machine;
- receipts for network and policy outcomes;
- snapshots, observations and native cursors;
- exact reconciliation of attempts without receipts;
- deterministic manifests and independent disposition.

## Later

C3 qualification, C4 target architecture, C5 executable master plan, C6 red-team, C7 final deterministic handoff, then product phases 12A-LIVE through 19.
